## Brand & Style
This design system defines an artisanal digital flagship for high-end bespoke perfumery, steeped in majestic dark aesthetics, regal poise, and oriental-occidental olfactory craftsmanship. Drawing inspiration from deep lacquered flacons, warm amber extraits, and polished packaging, the visual narrative embraces **Dark Luxury Minimalism with Skeuomorphic Gilded Restraint**. 

The digital atmosphere is moody, velvety, and commanding, evoking exclusivity, olfactory mystery, and heirloom quality. The experience caters to collectors, discerning fragrance connoisseurs, and gifting patrons who value bespoke formulations, premium longevity, and refined presentation. Spatial composition relies on expansive black canvases, tactile metallic accents, razor-thin champagne borders, and generous breathing room that lets bottle photography and olfactory notes take center stage.

## Colors
The palette evokes liquid gold poured against dark volcanic stone and black velvet display cushions. 

- **Primary (`#D4AF37`) & Secondary (`#E5C365`):** Warm antique gold and shimmering champagne brass. Used for prominent calls to action, emblem highlights, focused states, and selected indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#C5A059`):** Burnished metallic bronze for subtle borders, secondary badges, and delicate rules.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#0C0B0A`):** Obsidian black forming the core atmosphere, complemented by `#141311` (surface card container) and `#1E1C1A` (charcoal architectural borders and input backings).
- **Text & Contrast:** High-contrast editorial typography employs `#F9F6F0` (warm ivory) for headlines and lead copy, `#EDE8DF` (alabaster cream) for body text, and muted golden stone (`#A39E93`) for metadata, technical notes, and inactive labels.

## Typography
The typographic system pairs the dramatic contrast of **Playfair Display** with the architectural precision and clarity of **Montserrat**.

- **Editorial Serifs (Headlines & Slogans):** Playfair Display delivers high-contrast hairlines, traditional serif elegance, and dramatic gravitas for perfume names, collection titles, and sensory storytelling.
- **Architectural Sans-Serif (Body, UI, Pricing):** Montserrat provides an open geometric structure ensuring flawless legibility at small sizes. Used for sensory pyramids (top, heart, base notes), volume specifications (e.g., *100 ML Extrait de Parfum*), navigational structures, and transactional flows.
- **Currency & Pricing Treatment:** Currency representations formatted in Pakistani Rupees (`Rs.` or `PKR`) are tracked out with `label-price` styling to convey precision and prestige.
- **Small Caps & Metadata:** Collection headers, olfactive families, and button texts utilize uppercase tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.2em - 0.25em`) to evoke haute couture branding.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout philosophy is rooted in a refined 12-column desktop grid that transitions smoothly to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile. 

Generous negative space is deliberately preserved around product silhouettes and typographic lockups, reflecting the quiet confidence of ultra-luxury houses. Structural vertical rhythm utilizes 8px base increments. Margins expand on wide displays up to `4rem` (`64px`) to preserve a curated presentation frame, while sections breathe through expansive `space-xl` intervals. Grid gutters remain crisp and tight to allow photographic pairings of bottles and ingredient extracts to sit in balanced proximity.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth in this system avoids heavy blur drop-shadows, relying instead on **low-contrast metallic borders, tonal black layering, and warm inner radial glows**.

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - `Level 0 (Base Canvas):` `#0C0B0A` deep matte obsidian.
  - `Level 1 (Cards & Product Tiles):` `#141311` with a hairline border (`1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`).
  - `Level 2 (Dropdowns, Flyout Drawers, Modals):` `#1A1816` accompanied by a distinct burnished micro-border (`1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)`).
- **Gilded Illumination & Halo Shadows:** Premium interaction states emit a subtle, diffused warm champagne halo (`box-shadow: 0 8px 32px -4px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)`).
- **Hairline Dividers:** Section demarcations and header separations utilize 1px linear gradients running from `transparent` to `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.4)` to `transparent`, avoiding harsh boundaries while retaining luxury discipline.

## Shapes
A strict **Sharp (`0`)** shape language is implemented across all interactive elements, cards, and dialogue containers. 

Crisp right angles directly mirror perfume packaging boxes, heavy glass bottle pedestals, faceted crystal stoppers, and architectural fragrance display niches. Rectilinear precision signals tailored craftsmanship and timeless permanence. Tags, button frames, text fields, and thumbnail containers must maintain `border-radius: 0px`. Circular geometry is reserved strictly for avatar rings, swatch dots, or round perfume notes diagrams.

## Components

- **Buttons:**
  - *Primary Gold:* Background of antique gold gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #D4AF37 0%, #C5A059 100%)`), text in obsidian black (`#0C0B0A`), uppercase `title-sc` typography, sharp corners. On hover, transitions to a luminous champagne glow (`#E5C365`).
  - *Secondary / Outline:* Transparent fill with a 1px solid border of `#D4AF37`, text in warm ivory (`#F9F6F0`). Hover shifts background to `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`.
  - *Text Action:* Underlined with a 1px gold hairline sitting 4px below text, shifting right by 4px on hover.

- **Product Cards:**
  - Built on Level 1 surface (`#141311`) with a 1px hairline border in `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`.
  - Image frame features a subtle radial backdrop glow highlighting the perfume bottle silhouette.
  - Displays Olfactory Category in `label-caps` gold, Fragrance Name in `Playfair Display`, and Price in Pakistani Rupees (`Rs. 24,500`) in `label-price` format. Hover elevates the border opacity to `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.5)`.

- **Fragrance Note Accordion / Pyramid Chips:**
  - Notes (Top, Heart, Base) displayed in sharp rectangular chips with `#1E1C1A` fill, `1px solid rgba(229, 195, 101, 0.2)` stroke, and ivory typography.
  - Active note highlights border in rich gold with accompanying scent profiles.

- **Input Fields & Search:**
  - Sharp rectangular fields set against `#141311` with a bottom-only or fully enclosed 1px border in `#2A2724`.
  - Focus state transitions the border to `#D4AF37` with an ambient inner shadow. Placeholder text is rendered in `#736E66` Montserrat.

- **Checkboxes & Radios:**
  - Square boxes (0px radius) with `#141311` fill and 1px `#C5A059` border. Selected state reveals a centered solid gold square pip or checkmark.

- **Olfactory Pyramid Display Component:**
  - Custom brand component presenting the fragrance hierarchy (Head Notes, Heart Notes, Base Notes) aligned via horizontal gold hairlines with editorial serif titles and tactile icon indicators.
